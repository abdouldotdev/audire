## Description

Audire makes room for the books and documents you already own. Import an EPUB or PDF, choose a voice, and listen while keeping the text in view.

Carry on from the exact passage where you stopped, move through chapters with confidence, and tailor the page, spacing and voice speed to how you read best. Optional voice and translation models are chosen and installed by you.

Your library, settings and listening progress stay on your device. Some voice and translation options depend on the chosen model and device.
