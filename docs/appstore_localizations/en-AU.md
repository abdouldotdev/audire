## Description

Audire turns your EPUBs and PDFs into a focused reading and listening experience. Import a book, choose a voice, and resume from the exact passage where you stopped.

Keep the text in view while you listen, prepare chapters for smoother playback, and customise type size, spacing, theme, and speed. Your books, preferences, and progress stay on your device.

Some voice and translation options depend on the model you choose to install and on your device.
