## Description

Audire turns the EPUBs and PDFs you already own into a calm place to read and listen.

Import a book, choose a voice, and pick up from the exact passage where you left off. Follow the text as it plays, move between chapters without losing your place, and make long reading fit your commute, workout, chores, or quiet time.

- Import EPUB and PDF files from your device
- Listen with a phone voice or an optional installed voice
- Start playback from the passage you choose
- Follow the text while you listen
- Translate supported English passages into French with installed models
- Prepare a chapter or a book for smoother listening
- Keep books, settings, and reading progress on your device
- Adjust theme, type size, spacing, and playback speed to your comfort

Audire is built around local processing. Your documents are not sent to a reading service: your library stays in your control.

Some voice and translation options depend on the selected model and your device.
