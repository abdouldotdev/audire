## Description

Audire helps you turn the EPUBs and PDFs you already own into time you can read or listen to. Import a book, choose a voice, and carry on from the exact passage where you left off.

Follow the text as it plays, keep moving through chapters, and tailor the page, spacing, theme, and listening speed to your routine. Books, settings, and reading progress stay on your device.

Some voice and translation options depend on the model you choose to install and on your device.
